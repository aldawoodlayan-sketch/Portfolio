Odoo ERP E-Commerce

An academic enterprise systems project that simulated a grocery e-commerce business using Odoo ERP modules for products, CRM, sales, employees, social marketing, checkout, inventory, and dashboards.

OVERVIEW

Project summary

An academic enterprise systems project that simulated a grocery e-commerce business using Odoo ERP modules for products, CRM, sales, employees, social marketing, checkout, inventory, and dashboards.

Technologies

Odoo ERP CRM Sales E-Commerce Inventory Dashboards

HIGHLIGHTS

What the project included

  • Created an online grocery store and product catalog.
  • Added customer opportunities and managed the CRM pipeline.
  • Converted quotations into sales orders.
  • Configured cash on delivery and completed an online order workflow.
  • Created employee and social marketing records.
  • Reviewed sales and warehouse performance dashboards.
