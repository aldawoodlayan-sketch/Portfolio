Odoo ERP E-Commerce

Built a complete grocery e-commerce system using Odoo ERP, covering CRM, Sales, Inventory, Employees, and Dashboards.

OVERVIEW

Project summary

Academic project involved building a complete grocery e-commerce business in Odoo ERP. It covered product management, CRM, sales, employee records, inventory, social marketing, and business dashboards to simulate real business operations.

Technologies

Odoo ERP

HIGHLIGHTS

What the project included

  • Built an online grocery store with a product catalog.
  • Managed CRM opportunities and converted quotations into sales.
  • Completed the checkout and order workflow.
  • Monitored sales and inventory using dashboards.
